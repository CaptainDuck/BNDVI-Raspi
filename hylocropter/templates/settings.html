Camera
{% for w, h in resolutions %} {% endfor %}
Auto white balance and auto exposure stay locked during flight so captures stay comparable. JPEG is gamma-encoded, so the raw DNG is the honest input for calibration work — at the cost of about 10 MB per photo.

Neutralising the pipeline matters more than it sounds. Locking white balance still leaves the camera applying a colour-correction matrix that mixes the green channel into both red and blue — the channel this rig treats as blocked. A white card still reads zero, so calibration can't detect it, but vegetation readings shift by enough to move a plant across the healthy boundary. See RESEARCH-GAPS.md §4.
